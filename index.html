The weekly cycle

Learn → Practise → Assess → Revise → Improve

Students receive regular weekly assessments and revision to reinforce concepts, identify learning gaps and strengthen academic preparation.

Stage 1 of 5 LEARN

Building core mathematical and scientific concepts from foundational first principles. Ensuring deep conceptual understanding before memorisation.

  1. Understand

    Build the idea from first principles.

  2. Clarify

    Resolve doubts as they arise.

  3. Practise

    Apply it across varied problems.

  4. Assess

    Weekly checks reveal learning gaps.

  5. Improve

    Revise and strengthen, week by week.
